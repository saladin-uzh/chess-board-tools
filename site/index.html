Chess Board Tools

Keyboard controls and optional board helpers for Chess.com

Configure move confirmation shortcuts, enter squares with the keyboard, and use optional turn sound cues. Practice with Piece Vision Overlay against bots, in analysis, completed standard-game reviews, and Rated, Daily and Learning puzzles. Configure tick frequency and volume in the popup.

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Chess.com. Free, without advertising, purchases, or subscriptions.
